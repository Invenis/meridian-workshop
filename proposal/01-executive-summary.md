# Executive Summary

**RFP #MC-2026-0417 — Meridian Components Inventory Dashboard Modernization**
**Date:** April 28, 2026

---

Your operations team runs three warehouses and $9.6M in annual revenue through a dashboard that still doesn't work the way it should. The Reports module has known defects your previous vendor never resolved. The Restocking capability your team requested was never built. And because no tests were ever delivered, your IT team can't safely approve changes — which means every fix requires a risk conversation instead of a deployment.

This isn't a technology problem. It's a vendor execution problem. We're here to fix that.

**What we'll deliver — and what makes us different:**

Where the previous vendor left gaps, we start with ground truth. Before writing a line of code, we'll conduct a direct architecture review of the existing system — not a retread of incomplete handoff notes — so our scoping is accurate and our commitments are ones we can keep.

Where the previous vendor deferred testing, we treat it as a precondition. End-to-end browser test coverage (R3) comes before any change reaches production. That's not a line item for IT; it's how we work.

And where the previous vendor left the Restocking feature unbuilt, we'll deliver it: a purpose-built view that gives your operations team purchase order recommendations based on live stock levels, demand forecasts, and an operator-supplied budget ceiling — the tool your team has been waiting for.

**Our commitments:**

- **R1 — Reports remediation:** All logged defects resolved, including filter behavior, i18n gaps, and data inconsistencies
- **R2 — Restocking recommendations:** New capability, delivered and tested, before Q3 close
- **R3 — Automated browser testing:** Coverage in place before any changes are approved for production
- **R4 — Architecture documentation:** A current-state overview your IT team can actually use — produced from the codebase, not recycled from the previous vendor's notes

Fixed milestones. Defined deliverables. No surprises at the end.
